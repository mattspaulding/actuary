# User evidence

We keep this candid. Actuary is two days old at submission and its user
base is machines — which is the point of the category it competes in, but
we do not inflate what exists:

- **Users today:** the Actuary probe agent itself (buyer + seller sides,
  ~48 probe rounds/day autonomously) and the operator's development/demo
  agent, whose 4 paid lookups are disclosed as related-party in the P&L.
- **External users:** none yet at submission time. The adoption artifact
  is shipped and free: the `check-before-you-pay` agent skill
  (github.com/mattspaulding/actuary/tree/master/skills/check-before-you-pay)
  installs into any Circle-CLI-capable agent in one step.
- **Demographics, testimonials:** not applicable at this stage; the
  leaderboard's public counters (probes on record, days unattended, USDC
  earned) serve as the live usage record, verifiable by judges at
  https://actuary-695835808761.us-central1.run.app.

What we ask judges to weigh instead of user counts: every "usage event" in
this product is a settled USDC payment with an on-chain receipt — a form
of usage evidence that cannot be padded.
